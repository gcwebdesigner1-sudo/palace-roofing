What we do

Roofing
services

Homes and businesses across Nampa, Boise, Meridian and the rest of the valley. Every job starts with a free inspection and a written quote.

01

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off down to the deck. We fix bad sheathing, then install new underlayment, flashing and shingles rated for Idaho snow and summer heat.

02

Roof Repair

Missing shingles, a leak over the kitchen, a vent boot that cracked. We find the cause and fix it so it stays fixed.

03

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and exposed-fastener systems. Sheds snow fast, shrugs off fire and sun, and can outlast two or three shingle roofs.

04

Storm Damage

Hail and wind damage inspections with photos you can hand to your insurance adjuster.

05

Roof Inspections

A real walk on the roof, with pictures and a plain-English report of what's good, what's worn, and what can wait.

06

Roof Maxx Restoration

We're certified Roof Maxx installers. The plant-based treatment can add years to shingles that are dry but still sound.

07

TPO & Flat Roofs

Single-ply TPO for low-slope and commercial roofs, heat-welded seams and clean edge detail.

08

Commercial Roofing

Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings. We work around your hours and keep the site clean.

09

Siding & Fascia

Rotted fascia, loose soffit and damaged siding repaired so water stays out of the walls.

10

Emergency Repair

Tree came down or a storm tore off a section? Call and we'll get it covered fast.
